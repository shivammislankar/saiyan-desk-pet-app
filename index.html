A tiny fighter for your desktop.

He floats above your windows, throws a punch every time you type, fires an energy beam when you ask and keeps your to-do list. Free for Windows.

Download for Windows Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit

No install? Get the portable version. Mac builds are on the releases page.

Click here and type. Space kicks, Enter uppercuts, tap Alt twice to charge up.

Click, then type

He fights along, then gets out of the way.

Fights while you type

Every key is a punch or a kick. Space kicks, Enter throws an uppercut, Backspace blocks.

Charges an energy beam

Tap Alt twice. He chants, charges and fires a beam across your screen. It's only visual, so nothing you click is affected.

Keeps your to-do list

Click him and a small list opens beside him. Add, tick off and edit tasks. They're saved between restarts.

Stays out of your way

He stands still when idle and never steals focus. Drag and throw him anywhere; he remembers where you left him.

Install in a minute

  1. Download and open the installer

    Run Saiyan Desk Pet Setup and pick where to install it. The portable version needs no install at all.

  2. Get past the blue Windows warning

    Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed, which is normal for small free apps. Click More info, then Run anyway. Some antivirus tools also flag apps that listen to the keyboard.

  3. Say hi

    He appears in the middle of your screen. Find the orange icon near the clock (it may be under the ^ arrow) to turn on sound or quit.
